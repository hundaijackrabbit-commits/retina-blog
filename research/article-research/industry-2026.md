# Industry 2026 — Source & Claim Map

Last reviewed: August 11, 2026

This note supports the first in-house Retina.blog Industry expansion. The articles are original editorial synthesis. Source language should not be copied into article prose; sources are used to establish facts, regulatory status, intended use, trial status, and technical context.

## Editorial rules used

- Prefer primary regulatory records and trial registries for status claims.
- Label manufacturer material as manufacturer information.
- Do not treat FDA clearance as proof of clinical superiority.
- Do not treat company conference material as independent clinical evidence.
- Do not turn trial enrollment or Phase 3 status into a prediction of approval.
- Do not provide investment, prescribing, reimbursement, or individualized medical advice.
- Recheck time-sensitive regulatory and trial-status claims before future updates.
