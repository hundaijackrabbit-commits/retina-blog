# Retina.blog Research Library

This directory contains the research foundation for Retina.blog articles. All content claims should be traceable to verifiable sources documented here.

## Structure

### `/topic-notes/`
Research notes organized by major topic area:
- OCT and OCTA imaging
- AI and machine learning in ophthalmology
- Retinal diseases (DR, AMD, RD, IRD)
- Gene and cell therapy
- Oculomics and systemic biomarkers
- Clinical trials and regulatory developments
- Industry and technology developments

### `/article-research/`
Specific research conducted for individual articles, including:
- Source evaluation
- Evidence assessment
- Study limitations
- Citation details (DOI, PMID, URLs)

### `source-index.json`
Structured database of all sources used across the site, enabling:
- Citation reuse
- Evidence auditing
- Source quality tracking
- Article-to-source mapping

## Research Standards

### Source Quality Hierarchy
1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses (peer-reviewed)
2. Randomized controlled trials (peer-reviewed)
3. Prospective cohort studies (peer-reviewed)
4. Retrospective studies (peer-reviewed)
5. Case series (peer-reviewed)
6. FDA/NIH/official regulatory documents
7. ClinicalTrials.gov registry entries
8. Manufacturer/institutional announcements (for product/technology context only)

### What We Do NOT Cite
- Invented papers, DOIs, or PMIDs
- Search result snippets without verification
- Press releases as evidence (only as context)
- Unverified claims
- Fabricated statistics

### Citation Requirements
For each source used, record when available:
- **Title**: Full paper/document title
- **Authors**: Lead author at minimum, full list preferred
- **Journal/Institution**: Where published
- **Date**: Publication date
- **DOI**: Digital Object Identifier
- **PMID**: PubMed ID (if applicable)
- **URL**: Stable link to source
- **Study Type**: RCT, cohort, case series, review, etc.
- **Peer Review Status**: Published, preprint, regulatory doc
- **Key Findings**: What the study showed
- **Limitations**: What it did not show or study limitations
- **Used In**: Which Retina.blog articles cite this source

## Evidence Labeling

Articles use evidence status labels based on:
- **Established**: Multiple high-quality studies, systematic reviews, clinical adoption
- **Supported**: Good evidence but may lack extensive external validation
- **Emerging**: Early evidence, small studies, or limited replication
- **Preliminary**: Very early research, preprints, proof-of-concept only
- **Investigational**: Primarily from trials or research settings, not clinical standard

Labels should reflect research maturity, NOT marketing hype.

## Maintenance

This research library should be updated whenever:
- New articles are added
- Existing articles are updated
- New evidence becomes available
- Sources are deprecated or retracted
